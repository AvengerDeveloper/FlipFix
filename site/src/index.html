FlipFix Source on GitHub

For Galaxy Z Flip phones on One UI 9

Keep your cover video playing after you unlock.

One UI 9 pauses the video on your cover screen the moment you unlock, and covers it with widgets. FlipFix keeps the video playing, with your clock, notifications and music controls on top.

Latest version · Free and open source · Works offline

Experimental. Built and tested by one developer on two phones, and Samsung updates can break it. Read the disclaimer before installing.

Install in five steps

You need your phone, a USB cable that carries data, and a computer with Chrome or Edge. It takes about five minutes, and you only do it once.

This browser can't talk to your phone. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS computer. Firefox, Safari and phone browsers don't support it.

  1. Turn Auto Blocker off

    On your phone, open Settings›Security and privacy›Auto Blocker and turn it off. Samsung blocks USB setup while it's on. You'll turn it back on at the end.

  2. Turn on Developer options

    Open Settings›About phone›Software information and tap Build number seven times, until the phone says Developer mode is on. Enter your PIN or pattern if it asks.

  3. Turn on USB debugging

    Open Settings›Developer options and turn on USB debugging.

  4. Connect and install

    Keep your phone open and unlocked. Plug it into this computer, then click Connect phone and pick your phone in the list your browser shows. When your phone asks Allow USB debugging?, tap Allow.

    • Connect to your phone
    • Check it's a Z Flip on One UI 9
    • Install FlipFix
    • Give FlipFix its two permissions

  5. Finish on your phone

    • Turn USB debugging off, and Auto Blocker back on.
    • Open FlipFix once. Allow notifications when it asks, and add its Quick Settings tile.
    • Recommended: Settings›Apps›FlipFix›Battery, then pick Unrestricted, so Samsung doesn't put FlipFix to sleep.

    Then fold your phone, wake the cover screen and unlock. Your video keeps playing.

If something goes wrong

My phone isn't in the browser's list

Check that USB debugging is on (step 3) and that Auto Blocker is off (step 1). Some cables only charge: try another cable, or another USB port. Unplug the phone and plug it back in, then click Connect phone again.

The page says the phone is busy

Another program on this computer is using the phone's USB debugging connection: Android Studio, scrcpy, or a running adb. Close it (or run adb kill-server), unplug the phone and plug it back in, then try again. Only one browser tab can use the phone at a time.

My phone never asked "Allow USB debugging?"

Open and unlock the phone: the question shows on the main screen. If it still doesn't show, unplug the phone, open Settings›Developer options, tap Revoke USB debugging authorizations, plug it back in and click Connect phone again.

It says a different FlipFix is installed

A copy of FlipFix signed with another key is on the phone, for example one you built yourself. The page offers to remove it and install this one. Removing it clears FlipFix's settings, nothing else.

How do I remove FlipFix?

Settings›Apps›FlipFix›Uninstall. That removes FlipFix, its permissions and its settings.

What this page does

GitHub hosts this page and the FlipFix download. Everything else runs in your browser, which talks to your phone only over the USB cable and sends nothing anywhere. On your phone it installs FlipFix and runs the same two commands as the manual guide:

pm grant io.github.avengerdeveloper.flipfix android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS
cmd notification allow_listener io.github.avengerdeveloper.flipfix/io.github.avengerdeveloper.flipfix.FlipNotificationListener

The first lets FlipFix switch its accessibility service off when you open your phone, so banking apps don't complain. The second lets it show your notification icons and music controls on the cover. FlipFix itself has no internet access. So your phone recognises this browser next time, the browser keeps a key it made for this page. Revoke USB debugging authorizations in Developer options makes the phone forget it.

Prefer the command line? Download the APK and follow the manual steps.
